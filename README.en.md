# MARAZAWA

**Learning in cities. Meaningful spaces in villages.**

MARAZAWA is an emerging ecosystem connecting education, entrepreneurship, architecture and local community development, originating in Iran. Its approach starts with field observation and community dialogue, then moves toward a small, practical intervention, local operation and measurable follow-up.

Digital products and educational services, together with corporate partnerships, are intended to support a financially sustainable model. Reusing vacant and underused spaces is considered alongside new construction.
